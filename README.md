# EU AI Act — Classify Your Product Lab

## Lab Objective

This lab practices a consulting workflow for assessing AI systems under the EU AI Act.

The exercise covers:

- Prohibited AI practices
- High-risk AI systems
- Limited-risk systems and transparency obligations
- Minimal-risk AI systems
- Provider vs deployer roles
- AI architecture and human oversight
- Compliance controls
- Approval and redesign decisions

## Lab Workflow

1. Created four hidden client scenarios covering the four AI Act outcomes.
2. Prepared a private answer key.
3. Shared unlabeled client briefs with the partner.
4. Reviewed the partner's scenarios as a consultant.
5. Produced an approval recommendation for each case.
6. Compared the consultant classifications with the partner's intended categories.
7. Completed the final client discussion and sign-off.
